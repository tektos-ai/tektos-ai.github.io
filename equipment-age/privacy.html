Equipment Age privacy policy

Tektos AI LLC. Effective October 2, 2026.

Equipment Age reads the nameplate on HVAC equipment, water heaters and appliances and estimates when the unit was made. This page says what the app does with the information it handles.

What stays on your device

The app reads nameplate text on your iPhone or iPad. Each saved scan holds the photo, the text read from it, the brand, model, serial number and dates the app found, the name you entered at sign-in, the date and time of the scan, and the location where it was taken if you allowed location access. All of it is stored on your device. Tektos AI does not receive it.

Deleting a scan in the History tab removes it. Deleting the app removes every scan.

What leaves your device

  • The recall check sends the brand name to the US Consumer Product Safety Commission's public recall service at saferproducts.gov.
  • When location is allowed, the app sends the scan's coordinates to Apple's address lookup service to show a street address.
  • An export leaves your device only when you share it, and goes only where you send it.

What the app does not do

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The access code you enter at sign-in is checked on your device and is not sent anywhere. Tektos AI does not sell or share personal information.

Permissions

The camera reads nameplates. Photo library access lets you choose an existing photo. Location records where a scan was taken. Each is optional, and the app works without location.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date. Questions go to krishna@tektos-ai.com.
