Equipment Age support

Tektos AI LLC

Equipment Age reads the nameplate on HVAC equipment, water heaters and kitchen and laundry appliances. It finds the brand, model and serial number and estimates the manufacture date from the serial number, using decoding rules for about 600 brands.

Signing in

Enter your name and the access code your organization gave you. Your name is written on each scan you save.

Getting a good reading

  • Fill the frame with the label and hold the phone square to it.
  • Use the flashlight or move to avoid glare on metal plates.
  • Pick the equipment category before scanning when the plate does not say what the unit is.
  • If a value is wrong, correct the brand or serial under "Fix the reading" on the result screen.

Reading the result

"Exact match" means the brand was read from the plate and the serial decoded by a rule checked against the maker's documentation. "Approximate" means check the plate by hand: the rule is inferred, several styles disagree, or the decade is uncertain. "Not found" means no serial style matched; a date printed on the plate, if any, is still shown.
